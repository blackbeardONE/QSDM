Why QSDM

Six facts, each one checkable on the live network or in the source.

  • Post-quantum signaturesWallets sign with ML-DSA-87 (NIST FIPS 204), a lattice scheme designed to resist quantum attacks.
  • Fixed, published supplyThe cap is 90,000,000 CELL and the block reward halves every 12,623,040 blocks (about four years at 10 s).
  • GPU mining with attestationMiners enroll an NVIDIA GPU through QSDM Hive and earn CELL for accepted proof-of-work in capped windows.
  • Self-custody by defaultKeys are generated and kept on your device; Hive and the web wallet sign locally and send only signed transactions.
  • Open, keyless APIStatus, blocks, receipts, balances and miner enrollments are public HTTP reads with no account or API key.
  • Public auditAn open checklist with live pass/pending counts is published at /audit.html, and the source is MIT-licensed on GitHub.
