What the network sees

QSDM Network receives requests signed on your device and verifies them. It never receives your private key.

QSDM Network receives
Public addresses, public chain queries, and signed action envelopes.
Hive retains
Keystore JSON, passphrase, local approval state, and private signing operations.
Websites receive
The connected public address and only the signatures explicitly approved by the user.
Validators enforce
Signature validity, sender binding, nonce/replay checks, balances, stake, and consensus rules.
